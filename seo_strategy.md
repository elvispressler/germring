# SEO Strategy

- Public German-language local-news pages for germring.de: homepage, category archives, article detail pages, newsletter signup, and public static assets.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated or internal tools, API-only endpoints, and the mockup preview route unless it is exposed as public content.

## Target audience
- Residents of Fürstenfeldbruck (FFB) and surrounding communities interested in renewable energy, municipal politics, climate data, and local future topics.

## Primary keywords
- Unknown — likely German local-news queries around Fürstenfeldbruck, erneuerbare Energien, Kommunalpolitik, Klimaschutz, and local projects.

- Public pages should be indexable, shareable, and visible in initial HTML to search, social, and AI crawlers.
